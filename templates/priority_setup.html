Privacy and notifications

If you enable notifications, your device saves a push subscription (an identifier and encryption keys) so Agenndar can send you task reminders — never the content of your tasks themselves. You can turn this off at any time from the profile menu on your dashboard. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for how your data is handled.

Reset or delete your account

Wipes everything back to a blank slate: every task, every priority tier, and every counter (Total Logged, Done, Missed, Pushed, Behaviour Index) — restored to the same defaults as a brand-new account. Your login and display theme are kept. This cannot be undone.

Permanently deletes your account — every task, every priority tier, and your login itself. There is no recovery. You'll need to register again from scratch to use Agenndar in future.
